<CardGrid>
  <Card title="A real app window" icon="laptop">
    Its own taskbar entry and icon, a system tray icon if you want one, and single-window mode. Start it as a
    window, minimized or only in the tray.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Separate logins" icon="seti:lock">
    Every app keeps its own cookies and storage, so a work and a personal account can run side by side.
  </Card>
  <Card title="One command or a few clicks" icon="rocket">
    `penates create github.com` or the manager's Create tab: the name and icon come from the site.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Smart links" icon="external">
    Links to other sites open in your browser; sign-in popups stay in the app. Alt+← and the mouse's side
    buttons go back and forward.
  </Card>
  <Card title="An app catalog" icon="star">
    Known sites get small fixes, such as keeping sign-in inside the window. Add one in a click, and share
    your own fixes back.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Yours to keep" icon="open-book">
    MIT licensed, signed updates, no account and no telemetry. Export your apps to a file and take them anywhere.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>
