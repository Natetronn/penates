These sites are in the [app catalog](https://github.com/Natetronn/penates-apps). Add one from the manager's
**Create → Popular apps**, and its fixes apply automatically. Any other site works too: paste its address
under Create.

<CatalogList />

## Add a site

In the manager, an app's **⋮ → Share with the app catalog…** shows the entry for review and opens GitHub with
it filled in. Or `penates submit <id> --notes "what it fixes"`. See [Updates](../docs/updates/) for how
presets apply.
